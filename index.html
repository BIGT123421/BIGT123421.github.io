Hazy

Hazy is a personal AI assistant for Artin Hatami. It runs on his own Mac and helps him manage his work as a video editor.

What Hazy does

Hazy helps Artin keep up with work that arrives in his own Gmail account. With his permission it can read messages, organize them with labels, and prepare draft replies. Hazy cannot send email. It does not access Google Drive, Sheets, Docs, or Calendar.

The assistant stays on his computer. Nothing it reads is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Who it is for

Hazy is for Artin Hatami only (Hatami Media, Norway). It is a personal, non-commercial tool. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google account

Hazy connects only to Artin’s own Google account, and only because he allowed it. How that data is used, stored, and deleted is described in the Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at Google Account permissions.
